Coptic Dictionary guide

Search and Study Coptic Words

Search a structured Coptic lexicon from the script, transliteration, or English meaning you know, then examine detailed entries and turn useful words into a focused study set.

Published July 28, 2026 · Updated July 29, 2026 · Windows, Android

Coptic Dictionary is more than a one-line translation box. It is a searchable reference built around dictionary entries: headwords, English meanings, transliterations, grammatical information, dialect labels, written forms, references, and study tools. The search helps you approach the lexicon from the information you already have.

Search from Coptic, transliteration, or English

You can begin with a Coptic word, a transliterated form typed with a familiar keyboard, or an English meaning. Keep the first query focused: one word or a short expression usually makes the result list easier to judge than a full sentence.

  • Use Coptic script when you are reading a text directly.
  • Use transliteration when you know the sound or type-as form but not the keyboard layout.
  • Use an English meaning when you are trying to find possible Coptic equivalents.

Read the result list before choosing an entry

Several words can resemble one another or share an English gloss. Do not assume the first result is automatically the intended word. Compare the complete headword, meaning, grammatical type, dialect, and other displayed details before opening or saving an entry.

Use the detailed entry as a reference

A detailed entry can provide more context than a simple translation: transliteration, grammatical classification, dialect information, written forms, references, and related information where available. These details matter when two entries look similar but belong to different forms or traditions.

Filter a large dictionary

Use the available filters to narrow the searchable lexicon when the result set is broad. Depending on the entry data, you can refine by dialect, word type, study-ready status, or Greek origin. Filters are especially useful when a common English meaning returns several possible Coptic words.

Build a vocabulary set you will actually review

Save words connected to a reading, lesson, prayer, manuscript passage, or study topic. A smaller purposeful list is easier to remember than a large unsorted collection. The study area supports saved vocabulary, practice or quiz work, and copying or exporting study material for use outside the app.

  • Save the exact entry, not only the English meaning.
  • Keep dialect and grammatical details visible when they affect interpretation.
  • Review saved words in short, repeated sessions.

Useful for learners and serious readers

The searchable dictionary can support beginners learning vocabulary, readers identifying words in Coptic texts, clergy and students preparing lessons, and researchers comparing Sahidic, Bohairic, or other labelled material. It does not replace grammatical study, but it gives you a faster way to move from an unfamiliar form to a documented dictionary entry.
