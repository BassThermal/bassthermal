Favicon Harvester privacy

Favicon Harvester uses a local workspace while requesting public website resources.

What stays on the device

The input list, review state, accepted files, and exported collection remain in the local workspace and folders you choose.

What may be requested online

The domains or URLs you ask the app to inspect are requested from the corresponding public websites and hosting services so favicon candidates can be discovered.

What BassThermal receives

BassThermal does not receive your saved favicon collection through normal application use.

Store and platform services

Microsoft, Google, the operating system, and external services used by a requested feature may process purchase, installation, crash, update, network, or service-request information under their own policies.

Removing local data

Delete the local project or exported files and remove application data or uninstall the app to clear local settings.
