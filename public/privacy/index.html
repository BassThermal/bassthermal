Privacy policies

Data flows differ by product. Choose the application and platform you use to see what stays on your device and what may be requested online.

Website visits

BassThermal records basic website activity such as pages viewed, approximate location, device type, referring site, and clicks to product or Store pages. This helps show which applications and Guides are being used. Raw IP addresses and full browser identification strings are not stored.

A temporary same-day identifier is used to avoid counting every open tab as a different visitor.
