Privacy policies

Data flows differ by product. Review the policy for the specific application and platform you use.

Website visits

BassThermal records page visits, visible-page heartbeats, approximate country and city, device category, referrer host, and meaningful product or Store-link clicks. This is used to understand which pages and applications are being used. Raw IP addresses and full user-agent strings are not stored; a daily rotating visitor identifier is used to distinguish visitors for same-day reporting.
