DualTicker privacy

DualTicker requests public feeds and publisher websites to show headline content.

What stays on the device

Views, source choices, and application settings are handled by the platform or application where you use them. DualTicker does not require access to your private working documents.

What may be requested online

The app requests the public feed, page, image, or article resources needed for the sources you choose. Those requests are sent to the relevant publishers or hosting services.

What BassThermal receives

BassThermal does not receive your private documents or passwords through normal DualTicker use. Website activity on bassthermal.com is described in the website privacy section.

Store and platform services

Microsoft, Google, the operating system, and external services used by a requested feature may process purchase, installation, crash, update, network, or service-request information under their own policies.

Removing local data

Remove saved views or settings through the application where available, clear browser data for the web version, or uninstall the application to remove its local data.
