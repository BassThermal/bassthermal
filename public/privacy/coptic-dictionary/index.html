Privacy Policy — Coptic Dictionary

Updated November 24, 2026

Dictionary and study data

Coptic Dictionary is a reference and study app published by BassThermal. No account is required. Normal dictionary lookup and saved study data are handled on the device, as described in the app's documentation. Search history, saved vocabulary, study progress and preferences may be stored locally where supported. BassThermal does not receive saved vocabulary or study history through normal app use.

Advertising on Android

On Android, Coptic Dictionary displays banner ads through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process IP addresses, device or advertising identifiers, app interactions, diagnostics and ad performance data for delivery, measurement, security and fraud prevention. Processing depends on region, consent, device settings and Google’s policies. See Google's Privacy Policy and Google's advertising technologies information.

Other services and data choices

Store, update and external-link services may make their own requests according to their policies. You can remove local app data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it, and manage advertising choices through available device and Google privacy settings. BassThermal does not sell personal data.

Contact

For questions, contact info@bassthermal.com.
