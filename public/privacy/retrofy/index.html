RetroFy privacy

RetroFy processes source images and generated output on the device.

What stays on the device

Images, settings, previews, and exported results are handled locally by the application and saved only where you choose.

What may be requested online

Normal image processing does not require sending the source image to BassThermal. Store and operating-system services may make their own network requests under their policies.

What BassThermal receives

BassThermal does not receive your source images or exported results through normal application use.

Store and platform services

Microsoft, Google, the operating system, and external services used by a requested feature may process purchase, installation, crash, update, network, or service-request information under their own policies.

Removing local data

Delete exported files from the location where you saved them and remove application data or uninstall the app to clear local settings.
