GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked questions.

How the application works, what it needs and what is available now.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. Your inventory and everyday bookstore records are stored locally on your Windows computer. Looking up new book information and covers from online sources needs a connection.

Can I add a book without an ISBN?

Yes. Enter books manually when no ISBN is available. You can also record other items during a sale.

Does it process card payments?

No. ISBN Manager records a sale and the payment method used. It doesn’t connect to card terminals or charge customers’ cards.

Can I organize books into Catalogs?

Yes. Assign books to optional Catalogs or leave them uncatalogued. All Books remains a view of the entire inventory.

Can I import a spreadsheet?

The updated application can import TXT ISBN lists, CSV, TSV and XLSX files. Download a blank file or an example. Check the Microsoft Store listing for features available in the published version.

What if I use an older version?

Keep your current installation and library files. Follow the official upgrade instructions when a compatible update is released rather than replacing your existing data manually.
