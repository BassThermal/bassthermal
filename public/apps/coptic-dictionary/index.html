Coptic Dictionary

Search Coptic text, transliteration, English meanings, forms, and saved vocabulary.

Windows Android

what it does

Coptic Dictionary is a searchable lexicon and study tool. It helps you approach an entry from Coptic text, a transliterated form, or an English meaning, then inspect the details attached to the matching dictionary record.

how it works

  1. Search with Coptic text, transliteration, or a focused English term.
  2. Compare matching entries, meanings, grammatical information, dialect labels, and written forms where available.
  3. Save useful entries and review them as a purposeful vocabulary set.

useful for

  • looking up unfamiliar words while reading Coptic text
  • finding possible Coptic entries from an English meaning
  • comparing transliterations, forms, and labelled dialect information
  • building a saved vocabulary list for repeated study

requirements + limits

  • The app is a searchable lexicon and study tool, not an automatic sentence-translation service.
  • Dictionary coverage and entry detail depend on the available lexicon data.
  • Similar English meanings can point to different Coptic words, forms, or dialects and still require human judgement.
