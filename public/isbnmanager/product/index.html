Product

One home for the work behind the books.

ISBN Manager combines the essentials of book cataloging with practical stock and bookstore workflows in a focused Windows application.

Designed for clarity

The app has one job: help you run yours.

ISBN Manager follows the shape of real bookstore work: find a title, receive a delivery, sell a book, check stock, print labels, and understand what's changed.

  • Consistent navigation between core tasks.
  • Dense but readable working views.
  • Purpose-built controls rather than sprawling forms.
  • A careful distinction between local business data and public information.
