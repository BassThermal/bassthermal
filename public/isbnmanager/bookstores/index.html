For participating bookshops

Your shop. Your details. Your decision.

The desktop app's Store settings would prepare the public profile. The website displays only the fields approved for publication.

01

Prepare

Opt in inside ISBN Manager. Choose a display name, logo source, location, and optional links or contact details.

02

Review & verify

See exactly what would be shared. Submit with consent; publication follows ownership and content verification.

03

Stay in control

Update or withdraw the listing. Public pages must never expose inventory, customers, receipts, or private account data.

The attached desktop prototype only exports a draft JSON request; it does not submit listings to a server.

What visitors would see

Made to spotlight the bookstore—not the software.

Clean, consistent cards. A proper detail page. A direct route to the bookstore's own website. No cluttered reviews, invented ratings, or intrusive promotion.

  • Public business name and chosen image.
  • Optional city, country, website, and description.
  • Optional address, email, or phone only when explicitly selected.
