Billing principles

Good software should be predictable.

Protect previous purchases

A transition plan must honor the functionality users already purchased. No silent loss of access.

Offline continuity

A temporary outage must not unexpectedly erase entitlement to locally available paid tools. Exact rules remain to be defined.

Billing for our software

Future Stripe integration would charge for ISBN Manager licenses, not payments collected by bookstores from their customers.

How much does ISBN Manager cost?

Final pricing for the proposed website and expanded bookstore offering has not been set here. The pricing page is a structural concept and contains no live checkout.

What happens to people who already bought ISBN Manager?

Preserving existing customers’ purchased functionality is a product requirement. Exact migration, upgrade eligibility, and license rules must be written and verified before commercial launch.

Will the app work during an internet outage?

Offline continuity for paid features is a design requirement. The eventual implementation will need locally verifiable entitlements and carefully defined grace/expiry rules. This website concept does not implement or certify that behavior.
