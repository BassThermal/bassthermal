Account

One place for your ISBN Manager license.

This account experience is a forward-looking design for managing your software license and purchases. Sign-in and payments are not connected.

Non-functional account concept. No credentials are accepted and there is no payment form. The eventual Stripe integration would be for ISBN Manager billing, not purchases made by a bookstore's customers.

What belongs here eventually?

Product ownership, purchased feature rights, receipts and invoices, device activation controls, and transparent rules for working offline when the license server is unreachable.

What does not belong here?

A bookstore’s customer payment processing, personal shopping history, or an automatic copy of the shop’s private catalog.

A real account flow will need identity verification, secure sessions, access control, billing webhooks, and tested failure recovery.
